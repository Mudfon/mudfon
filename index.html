WHO WE ARE

Working together for a stronger Ummah.

The Muslim Ummah Development Forum of Nigeria (MUDFON) is a platform dedicated to promoting unity, knowledge, leadership and community development among Muslims in Nigeria.

We believe that when individuals and communities come together with a shared vision, they can create meaningful and lasting positive change.
